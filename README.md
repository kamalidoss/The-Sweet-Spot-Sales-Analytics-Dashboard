# 🍫 The Sweet Spot – Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The Sweet Spot is a business intelligence project focused on analyzing confectionery sales data using Power BI.  
It provides an interactive dashboard to monitor revenue, profit, regional performance, and product-level insights for better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall revenue and profit performance  
- Evaluate division-wise sales contribution  
- Understand regional revenue distribution  
- Identify top-performing products  
- Track monthly sales trends  

---

## 📊 Tools & Technologies
- **Microsoft Excel**
  - Data Cleaning
  - Data Transformation
  - Handling missing values
  - Removing duplicates  

- **Power BI**
  - Data Modeling
  - DAX Calculations
  - Interactive Dashboard Creation
  - KPI Tracking

---

## 🗂️ Data Source
Dataset: US Candy Distributor  
Source: https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/us-candy-distributor

---

## 🏗️ Data Model
- **Fact Table:** Sales  
- **Dimension Tables:** Products, Target, Date  

Relationships:
- Many-to-one relationships established  
- Proper cardinality maintained

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### 🔹 KPI Highlights
- Total Revenue: 93.44K  
- Profit Margin: 65.91%  
- Total Units Sold: 39K  
- Target Sales: 45K  

### 🔹 Key Findings
- Chocolate division is the primary revenue driver  
- Pacific region contributes the highest revenue  
- Few top products dominate overall sales  
- Some regions and divisions underperform

## 🔍 Insights

### Descriptive
- Strong profitability with high revenue margins  
- Chocolate category dominates sales  

### Diagnostic
- High-margin products drive overall profit  
- Regional demand varies significantly  

### Predictive
- Potential decline in sales trend if no action taken  
- High-performing regions likely to grow further  

### Prescriptive
- Invest more in chocolate products  
- Improve performance in low-performing regions  
- Use promotions to stabilize monthly sales
